# The home page leads with a slogan and two replayed demos

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `landing`
- **PR:** [#1321](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1321)

[中文版](2026-10-25-landing-story.zh.md)

The landing home page was re-cut around one story: a slogan hero, then two demos, A2APP and observable self-evolution, followed by the cost comparison, customer scenarios, the latest posts, the quick start and the close.

## Details

- **Hero.** The slogan "Software that keeps growing. Evolution within reach." (「让软件持续成长，让进化触手可及」) and the subtitle "A free, stable RSI framework for agents" (「免费、稳定的 Agent 自进化框架」) replaced the positioning line, its badge, the tagline, the stat tiles and the count-led catalogue rows. The provider logos end in a link, "and 1000+ models", to MMSP's model index in the site's language; the live start page stays under it. The page title, description, share card and footer tagline follow the slogan.
- **A2APP.** A scripted player replays one run per start-page starter (contract desk, docs Q&A, market board): one sentence builds the workbench, its tab appears beside Chat once it loads, the workbench calls the agent over the host API, and in the contract run one more sentence in Chat adds a column, hot-reloads the tab and leaves a second version in History. The legal-app recording with its cost line and a link to the "From A2UI to A2APP" post follow. The section replaced Cases; the footer's `/#cases` link became `/#a2app`, and a `/#self-evolution` link joined it.
- **Self-evolution.** A scripted player replays two rounds of the Default loop beside a scoreboard that only gains a point when a version is kept (round 2 is rolled back), followed by the RSI algorithm and Benchmark lists, the decision-agent recording with its cost line, and the docs links.
- **Removed.** The showcase rows, the loop diagram with its illustrative trends, the architecture diagram and the agent-library comparison left the page. The two recordings no longer carry a sentence retelling what they show.
- Both players are labelled as example runs, pause off-screen, and show their finished state without playing under reduced motion.
